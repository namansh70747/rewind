## Context

What is the problem, and what forces make a decision necessary? Capture the
constraints, requirements, and assumptions in play. This section should let a future
reader understand the situation *without* already knowing the answer. State facts and
pressures, not the decision itself.

## Decision

The decision, stated plainly and in the active voice: "We will …". Be specific enough
that someone can tell whether the system actually follows it. Include the key details
that make the decision actionable.

## Consequences

What becomes true once this decision is in force — both the benefits and the costs.
List what becomes **easier**, what becomes **harder**, and any new obligations or
risks the decision introduces. Honest downsides belong here.

## Alternatives Considered

The serious alternatives that were evaluated and **rejected**, each with a one- or
two-line reason for rejection. This is the most valuable section for a future reader:
it shows the decision was a choice among real options, not the only path considered.
